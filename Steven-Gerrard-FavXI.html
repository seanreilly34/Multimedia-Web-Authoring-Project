My Favourite Eleven of All Time

Steven Gerrard

"Stevie G"

From a local lad in Huyton to the heartbeat of one of football’s greatest clubs, Steven Gerrard embodied Liverpool Football Club like few players ever have. Born in 1980, he broke into the first team as a teenager and never looked back, captaining the side through its most dramatic modern era. Powerful, relentless and blessed with a thunderous strike, Gerrard could change games single-handedly. His performance in the 2005 Champions League final is the stuff of legend, dragging Liverpool back from the brink on the biggest stage. Despite never winning a Premier League title, his loyalty and influence only deepened his legacy. Gerrard wasn’t just a great midfielder — he was Liverpool.
