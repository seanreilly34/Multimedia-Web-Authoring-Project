My Favourite Eleven of All Time

ALESSANDRO NESTA

"The Wall"

From a teenage captain at Lazio to one of the most complete defenders ever, Alessandro Nesta combined elegance with absolute defensive mastery. Born in Rome in 1976, he rose through Lazio’s academy and led the club to Serie A glory before moving to AC Milan in 2002. Intelligent, composed and flawless in the tackle, Nesta defended with timing rather than force. At Milan, he formed legendary partnerships and was central to multiple Champions League triumphs. Injuries robbed him of longevity, but not greatness. At his best, Alessandro Nesta made defending look like an art form.
