My Favourite Eleven of All Time

PAOLO MALDINI

"Il Capitano"

From carrying one of football’s greatest surnames to becoming the gold standard for defenders, Paolo Maldini defined elegance, loyalty and longevity. Born in 1968, he spent his entire career at AC Milan, making his debut as a teenager and remaining at the top level for over two decades. Calm, intelligent and positionally flawless, Maldini rarely needed to tackle because he was always exactly where he needed to be. He captained Milan through an era of domestic and European dominance, winning multiple Serie A titles and five Champions Leagues. Equally brilliant at left-back or centre-back, he redefined defensive excellence. Maldini didn’t just defend, he made it look effortless.
