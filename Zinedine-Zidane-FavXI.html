My Favourite Eleven of All Time

ZINEDINE ZIDANE

"Zizou"

From being rejected by Algeria as a youngster for being too slow to then going on to be France's greatest player of all time, Zinedine Zidane's technical ability as a footballer is nothing short of the best ever. Born on June 23rd, 1972, in Marseille, Zidane’s career started in Cannes where he made 61 appearances, he then moved to Bordeaux where he truly burst onto the scene making 139 appearances and scoring 28 goals. Major European clubs all competed for his signature, but in 1996 he decided to join the current European champions Juventus. At his time in Turin, he became the best player in the world and a club legend making 151 appearances and scoring 24 goals. Finally in 2001, the biggest club in the world Real Madrid were unleashing a new transfer policy dubbed "The Galactico’s" and of course made the biggest statement they could possibly make by signing the Frenchman. For Real Madrid Zidane made 155 appearances scoring 37 goals including one of the greatest goals of all time against Bayer Leverkusen in the champions league final to secure the 2002 European title. But it actually for his national team France where he truly gained his legendary status, in the 1998 world cup which was hosted in France he stole the show, completed by an all-timer performance against a strong Brazil side who he completely dismantled. He won his nation their first ever world cup and earned himself the 1998 Ballon d'Or.
