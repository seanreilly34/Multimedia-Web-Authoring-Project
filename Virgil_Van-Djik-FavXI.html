My Favourite Eleven of All Time

VIRGIL VAN DIJK

"Virg"

From being released by Willem II as a teenager to becoming the best defender of his generation, Virgil van Dijk’s rise is one of modern football’s great stories. Born in Breda in 1991, he rebuilt his career at Groningen before making his name at Celtic, where his dominance quickly became impossible to ignore. A move to Southampton proved he belonged at Premier League level, but it was at Liverpool where he truly reached elite status. Calm, commanding and physically flawless, Van Dijk transformed Liverpool’s defence overnight. He was the backbone of their Champions League and Premier League triumphs, finishing second in the 2019 Ballon d’Or. More than just a centre-back, he became the standard by which modern defenders are judged.
