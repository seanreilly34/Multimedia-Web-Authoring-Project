My Favourite Eleven of All Time

LIONEL MESSI

"The Goat"

From a small boy in Rosario with a growth hormone deficiency to the greatest footballer the game has ever seen, Lionel Messi’s story feels almost unreal. Born in 1987, he left Argentina young to join Barcelona, where his talent quickly outweighed any physical doubt. What followed was an era of total dominance — goals, assists and moments that redefined what was possible on a football pitch. Quiet, humble and devastatingly effective, Messi combined vision, balance and finishing like no one before him. After years of international heartbreak, he finally crowned his legacy by leading Argentina to World Cup glory in 2022. More than trophies or numbers, Messi gave football magic — and made it look effortless.
