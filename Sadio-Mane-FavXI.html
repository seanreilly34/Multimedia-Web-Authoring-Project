My Favourite Eleven of All Time

Sadio Mane

"The Best Football Player in the World"

From a small village in Senegal to the very top of world football, Sadio Mané’s journey is built on sacrifice, speed and pure heart. Born in 1992 in Bambali, he left home young to chase a dream few believed in, first breaking through in France before exploding at Southampton. His move to Liverpool in 2016 unlocked his full potential, becoming one of the most devastating forwards in Europe. Relentless, selfless and lethal, Mané was central to Liverpool’s Champions League and Premier League triumphs. In 2019, he finished joint top scorer in the Premier League and later led Senegal to their first-ever AFCON title. More than just goals, Mané brought humility and hunger to everything he touched.
